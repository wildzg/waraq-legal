Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Effective: 17 November 2026

Sijill collects no personal data, transmits none, and shares none.

Sijill ("the App") is developed and published by the independent developer du james ("we").

1. We collect no data

Sijill collects, transmits and shares no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no server of ours that receives anything from your device. We do not know who you are, where you are, or how often you use the App.

2. Camera and photos

The App requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose a document to convert. The image is read by Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone. The image is never uploaded, never sent to us or to any third party, and is not retained after the temporary file needed to produce your export is no longer required. Temporary files are deleted when the export completes or when the App is closed.

3. No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The App contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of any kind. The App makes no network connections.

4. Data stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of information on your device only:

  • how many free exports remain, and whether you have unlocked the full version;
  • a local diagnostic log of the last 200 events (for example "OCR finished in 1.2 s"), which you can clear at any time in Settings.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the App deletes it.

5. Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction. You can restore the purchase at any time in Settings.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will confirm that we do not.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
