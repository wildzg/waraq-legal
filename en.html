Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Effective date: 11 November 2026

Waraq does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Waraq ("the app") is developed and published by the independent developer du james ("we", "us").

1. We do not collect any data

Waraq does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no server of ours that receives anything from your device. We do not know who you are, where you are, or how often you use the app.

2. Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose a document to scan. That image is read by Apple's on-device Vision framework on your iPhone. It is never uploaded, never transmitted to us or to any third party, and never stored beyond the temporary file needed to produce your export. Temporary files are deleted when the export completes or when you close the app.

3. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of information locally on your device only:

  • your remaining free exports and whether you have unlocked the full version;
  • a local diagnostic log of the last 200 events (for example "OCR run completed in 1.2 s"), which you can clear at any time in Settings.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

5. Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction. You can restore your purchase at any time from Settings.

6. Children

Waraq is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will confirm that we do not.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
